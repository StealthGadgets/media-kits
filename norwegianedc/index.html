01 / about

simple, sharp, built to last

Robin (@norwegian_edc) covers knives and everyday carry with a distinctly Nordic eye — gear tested in cold, wet, and low-light conditions, and reviewed with an emphasis on function over flash.

The audience values restraint: fewer, better pieces, chosen for reliability rather than hype. A recommendation carries weight here because it's been carried, not just photographed.

Partnerships are built around quiet, credible use — gear earns its place in the kit before it earns a mention.
