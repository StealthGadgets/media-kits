01 / About

Gear tested where it's used

Danny Sims (@dannysimsabi) covers tactical and outdoor gear from the field — backcountry setups, load-bearing equipment, camp systems, and firearms content shot in the environments they're actually built for.

The audience shows up for the same reason: they want to know how something performs under real weight, real weather, and real miles — not just how it looks in a flat-lay photo.

Partnerships are built around field-first content — gear goes out on trail or to the range before it shows up in a post.
