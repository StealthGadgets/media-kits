01 / About

Slow looks at sharp things

Chris Warras (@cwarras) covers knives and blades with a quiet, deliberate approach — steel, geometry, grind, and the way a blade actually feels in hand, given the attention a well-made object deserves.

The audience values that restraint: no rushed unboxings, no filler — just an honest, unhurried look at whether a piece is genuinely well made.

Partnerships are built around real attention — every blade is handled and considered before it's ever featured.
