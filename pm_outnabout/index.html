01 / About

Fit, finish, and follow-through

PM OutNAbout (@pm_outnabout) covers knives, blades, watches, and everyday carry gear with an eye for the details that separate a good piece from a great one — fit, finish, and how something actually holds up day to day.

The audience appreciates that range: they're as interested in a well-built pocketknife as they are in a well-built watch, and they trust a recommendation that's been worn and carried, not just photographed.

Partnerships are built around genuine daily wear — every piece is worn, carried, and used before it's featured.
