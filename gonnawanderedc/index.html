01 / About

Gear that goes the distance

Kim (@gonnawanderedc) covers knives and everyday carry through a travel lens — gear picked, packed, and tested on actual trips, not just laid out on a desk.

The audience is practical and mobility-minded: they want to know what survives a carry-on, what holds up across time zones, and what earns its spot when every ounce counts.

Partnerships are built around real travel use — pieces get packed and put to work before they show up in a post.
