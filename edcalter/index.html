01 / About

One blade, two perspectives

Content built around the tension between form and function.

Alter (@edc.alter) covers everyday carry knives with an eye for both sides of the object — the engineering that makes a blade work, and the everyday experience of actually carrying it.

The audience is thoughtful and detail-driven: they want to understand a knife before they commit to it, and they trust a perspective that looks past the marketing to the object itself.

Partnerships are built around considered use — every knife is carried, opened, and closed enough times to have an honest opinion about it.
