01 / About

Carried, not just unboxed

The Average Knife Guy (@theaverageknifeguy) covers knives and everyday carry for people who care about fit, finish, and how a piece actually holds up in the pocket — not just how it looks on a table.

The audience is detail-driven: they want lock-up quality, steel choice, and real carry impressions before they commit to a purchase, and they trust a recommendation that's spent real time in rotation.

Partnerships are built around honest carry time — every piece gets pocketed, used, and put through daily tasks before it's reviewed.
