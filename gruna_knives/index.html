FIG. 1 / About

Built well, and shown that way

Alex Gruna (@gruna_knives) covers knives, blades, torches, and everyday carry gear with the eye of someone who understands how things are built — grind, tolerance, materials, and the small decisions that separate good gear from great gear.

The audience wants that depth: they're not satisfied with a surface-level unboxing, they want to know how a piece is actually made and whether it holds up under real use.

Partnerships are built around real examination — every piece is handled, tested, and understood before it's featured.
