01 / About

Made by hand, tested in the dark

The Virg (@theabysslooksback001) covers handmade knives, knuckles, and one-off EDC gadgets with an eye for the craft behind them — fit, finish, and the kind of build quality that only comes from someone who actually makes things.

The audience shows up for that authenticity: they want to see a piece forged, ground, and finished, not just unboxed. A recommendation carries weight here because it comes from someone who understands what went into it.

Partnerships are built around craft and honesty — every piece is handled, used, and judged on its own merit before it's featured.
