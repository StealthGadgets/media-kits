01 / About

Tested under the trees

Stefan Rauh (@waldschrat_edc) covers knives, blades, and everyday carry gear with a quiet, woodsman's approach — every piece taken into the forest, used for real tasks, and judged on whether it holds up out there, not just on a table.

The audience shares that patience: they want gear that's dependable in damp, cold, and rough conditions, and they trust a review that comes from actual time spent outside.

Partnerships are built around honest field use — every piece is carried into the woods before it's ever featured.
