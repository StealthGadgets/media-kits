01 / About

Guns, straight up

Shadoshot covers firearms first: reviews, builds, and range breakdowns aimed at people who want to know how a gun runs, not just how it looks in a photo.

The audience is detail-oriented and gun-literate — they read the spec sheet, ask about reliability and fit, and trust a recommendation that's been fired, not just unboxed.

Partnerships are built around real range time — every firearm or accessory gets put through rounds before it shows up on camera.
