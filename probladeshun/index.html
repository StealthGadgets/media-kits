01 / About

Every blade, same test

ProBlades (@probladeshun) covers knives, blades, and everyday carry gear with a performance-first approach — every piece run through the same evaluation, so followers can compare gear the way they'd compare stats.

The audience values consistency: they trust a rating they can compare across reviews, and they come back because the standard never moves.

Partnerships are built around honest scoring — a product's rating reflects how it actually performs, not who sent it.
