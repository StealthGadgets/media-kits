01 / About

Real gear, no gloss

Brandon Spencer (@b_shady_22) covers knives, knuckles, and everyday carry gadgets with a straightforward, street-level approach — no filler, no fluff, just whether a piece actually does its job.

The audience trusts that directness: they want to know how something feels in the hand, how it holds up under daily wear, and whether it's worth carrying — not a polished sales pitch.

Partnerships are built around honest, everyday use — every product gets carried and put through real conditions before it's featured.
