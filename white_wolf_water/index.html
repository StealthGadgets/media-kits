01 / About

Tested in cold water, not a studio

Daniel Sierocki (@white_wolf_water) covers knives, blades, and everyday carry gear with an emphasis on how things perform in the elements — cold hands, wet steel, and the kind of conditions that separate dependable gear from the rest.

The audience trusts that lens: they want to know if a knife still locks up after a river crossing, not just how it looks on a table.

Partnerships are built around real-condition testing — every piece is carried outdoors before it's ever featured.
