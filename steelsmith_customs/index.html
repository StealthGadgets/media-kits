01 / About

From raw steel to finished edge

Josh Sipp (@steelsmith_customs) covers custom knives and blades from the shop floor — stock removal or forge work, grinding, heat treat, and final finish, all documented as it happens.

The audience wants that transparency: they're not just buying a finished object, they want to see the work that went into it, and they trust a maker who shows the process, not just the highlight shots.

Partnerships are built around real shop work — every piece is actually built, tested, and shown start to finish.
